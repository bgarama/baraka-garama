# Non-Regression Testing (NRT)

## Overview

Performed Non-Regression Testing (NRT) activities to verify that previously validated services and functionalities continued to operate correctly following software upgrades, platform updates, configuration changes, and defect fixes.

Testing was executed across telecommunications environments to ensure service stability, functionality, and deployment readiness.

---

## Objective

Verify that existing services remained unaffected by new software releases while ensuring platform stability and service continuity.

Key objectives included:

- Service stability validation
- Feature retention verification
- Defect re-validation
- Deployment readiness assessment
- Service continuity confirmation

---
