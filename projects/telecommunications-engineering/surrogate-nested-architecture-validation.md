# Surrogate & Nested Architecture Validation

## Overview

Performed validation activities for Surrogate and Nested service architectures within SIP-based telecommunications environments.

The purpose of this activity was to verify service functionality, registration behavior, routing logic, and interoperability across multi-layer communication architectures.

Testing focused on ensuring that voice services remained accessible and operational when subscriber requests traversed intermediary service layers before reaching the destination platform.

---

## Objective

Validate service delivery and SIP signaling behavior within complex routing architectures involving surrogate and nested service components.

Key objectives included:

- Registration validation
- Call routing verification
- SIP interoperability testing
- Service continuity validation
- Platform integration testing
- Release readiness support

## Architecture Overview

### Nested Architecture

In nested deployments, voice services traverse additional service layers before reaching the final service destination.

---

### Surrogate Architecture

Surrogate architectures introduce intermediary service components responsible for handling service requests and routing decisions.

Validation focused on:
