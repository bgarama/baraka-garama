# Baha Madzo Gadze Community Platform (Kenya)

## Overview

Contributed to the development, deployment, and maintenance of the official website for Baha Madzo Gadze, a community-based organization (CBO) located in Kilifi County, Kenya.

The organization focuses on environmental conservation, sustainable waste management, climate action, community empowerment, education, and public engagement initiatives.

The platform serves as the primary digital presence of the organization, providing visibility for its programs, projects, partnerships, and community activities.

---

## Objective

Design and maintain a professional digital platform capable of supporting the organization's mission while improving accessibility to information, community engagement, and awareness of environmental initiatives.

Key objectives included:

- Establish a professional online presence
- Improve access to organizational information
- Promote environmental and community initiatives
- Support stakeholder and partner engagement
- Create a scalable platform for future projects
