# Zero Plastic Football Fiesta (Kenya)

## Overview

Developed a digital platform supporting the Zero Plastic Football Fiesta initiative, a community-driven environmental awareness program operating under Baha Madzo Gadze in Kilifi County, Kenya.

The initiative uses sports as a vehicle for environmental education, climate action, sustainable waste management, and community participation.

The platform was designed to increase visibility, promote participation, support outreach activities, and provide information regarding the initiative and its environmental objectives.

---

## Objective

Create a modern digital platform capable of promoting environmental awareness while supporting community engagement and event participation.

Key objectives included:

- Promote environmental conservation
- Increase community participation
- Improve visibility of initiative activities
- Support fundraising and awareness campaigns
- Provide a scalable digital platform for future events

### Community Engagement

The platform needed to communicate environmental objectives in a clear and engaging way to diverse community audiences.

The solution needed to support future editions of the initiative and accommodate future content expansions.

### Deployment Architecture

The platform had to integrate cleanly into the existing Baha Madzo Gadze digital ecosystem while maintaining independent branding and visibility.
